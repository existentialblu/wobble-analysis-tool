NIGHT PORTRAITS

One for all of them.

ResMed PAP recordings only. Other manufacturers and summary-only OSCAR exports are not supported. Choose a ResMed DATALOG night folder. Only its BRP / PLD signal files are selected; you can also choose EDF files or a ZIP. Add more night folders to grow the gallery. Selecting the same recording date replaces that night.

Each portrait comes from a delayed polynomial map fitted to clean, non-overlapping eight-minute sections across that night. The state is locally normalised and sampled at a fixed five-second step. We extend the fitted map into complex numbers and explore an initial-state slice.

All nights fits pooled samples from the loaded nights, giving each night equal weight. It appears after two nights are loaded. It is a new fitted map; the PNGs are not blended together.

These are experimental mathematical portraits. Their intricate geometry depends on the chosen model, and the coefficients have no established clinical interpretation. Each recording includes the effects of its PAP therapy.

All processing happens locally. Nothing from your files is uploaded or retained after you close or reload the page. Save PNG exports the current fractal view without interface elements.
