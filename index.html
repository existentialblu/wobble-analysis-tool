What changed

Robust despiking

A rolling median/MAD filter suppresses isolated spikes without smearing breath shape.

Baseline correction

A slow adaptive baseline removes sensor drift before breath boundaries are detected.

Artifact-aware windows

Flatline, clipping, extreme noise, and implausible respiratory-rate windows are excluded and reported.

Stable periodicity

Hann-windowed, overlapping spectra reduce leakage and prevent a single transient from dominating the night.
